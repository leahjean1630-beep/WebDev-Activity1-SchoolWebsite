DEPED CURRICULUMNS

The Department of Education (DepEd) is implementing the MATATAG Curriculum, a revised K-10 basic education program designed to decongest the previous K-12 setup by 70% and focus heavily on foundational skills.

Junior Highschool(JHS)

ENGLISH, FILIPINO, MATHEMATICS,SCIENCE, AND ARALING PANLIPUNAN(Social Studues). The Program also integrates holistics and practical learning areas such as MAPEH (Music, Arts, Physical Education, and Health), Edukasyon sa Pagkakatao(ESP or Values Education), and Technology and Livelihood Education(TLE), which offers exploratory courses in home economics, agriculture, and ICT.

Senior Highschool(SHS)

Senior high school covers Grades 11 and 12, forming the final two years of basic secondary education.

Special Program in Technical Education(SPTVE)

SPTVE stands for the Special Program in Technical-Vocational Education, a curriculum track in Philippine secondary schools designed to give students practical, industry-aligned skills alongside core academic subjects.

Special Program

is a structured, targeted initiative designed to serve a specific group of people, address a unique need, or provide specialized training beyond a standard curriculum or routine operation.
